About Us

Cyberland is a family-owned and managed Theme Park located in the South East of England. Over the years, we have grown from a small park with only 12 employees to a nationally recognized destination, attracting over 230,000 visitors annually!

We have built a reputation for delivering quality entertainment in a safe environment for all ages. We sincerely believe in our motto: “Where the fun never stops!”
